01 / ABOUT ME

From solving customer problems to finding patterns in data.

My professional journey started in business operations and customer experience. At Honeywell, Capgemini, and FFIND, I worked with different teams, systems, and business questions. Over time, I became increasingly interested in the information behind the decisions we make.

Today, I'm studying Business Analytics at Brandeis University, developing my skills in Python, SQL, R, Tableau, and statistical analysis. I'm especially interested in AI, healthcare analytics, and how thoughtful technology can improve people's lives.

I'm also working toward a career in data engineering. I'm strengthening my Python and SQL foundations and learning how reliable data pipelines, data transformation, and well-organized data systems can support better analysis and decisions. This is the direction I'm growing into, not a role I claim to have mastered yet.

One goal that is especially personal to me is to invest time and research in improving quality of life for older adults. I want to explore how data, accessible technology, and AI might help people remain independent, feel connected, and receive better support as they age. This is a future research direction I am passionate about, and I hope to turn it into meaningful work.
