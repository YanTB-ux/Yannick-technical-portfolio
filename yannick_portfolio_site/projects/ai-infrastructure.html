THE QUESTION I WANT TO EXPLORE

What could the next generation of AI mean for the systems behind it?

I'm interested in the links between advanced AI, computing infrastructure, electricity use, cooling, reliability, and practical decision-making. The specific research question and scope are still to be defined.

POSSIBLE RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Questions worth studying

  • How increasing AI workloads may influence energy and computing needs.
  • What efficiency, reliability, and cooling challenges could emerge.
  • How organizations may weigh new AI capabilities against infrastructure constraints.
  • Which evidence would be useful for evaluating different future scenarios.
