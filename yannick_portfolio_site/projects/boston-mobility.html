THE BUSINESS QUESTION

How did visitation patterns differ across technology, university, and comparison locations in Boston and Cambridge?

Used place-of-interest visit data and Tableau maps to explore changes across technology, university, and comparison areas.

WHAT I WORKED ON

Approach and responsibilities

  • Worked with Advan point-of-interest mobility data for a course visualization project.
  • Created and interpreted Tableau geospatial views to compare visitation patterns across area types.
  • Compared time periods and considered the importance of choosing appropriate comparison groups.
  • Focused on communicating observed patterns clearly rather than making unsupported causal claims.
WHAT IT DEMONSTRATES

Why this work matters

The work demonstrates how location data and careful comparison can turn a broad policy or business question into a clear visual investigation.

This is an academic project. The visual shown on this page is a concept illustration, not a screenshot of project results.
