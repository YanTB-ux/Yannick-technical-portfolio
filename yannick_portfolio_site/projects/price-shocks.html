THE BUSINESS QUESTION

How might international conflict and economic uncertainty relate to changes in U.S. prices beyond gasoline?

Explored how major international events might be associated with changes in multiple categories of consumer prices.

WHAT I WORKED ON

Approach and responsibilities

  • Framed a research question around international shocks and domestic prices.
  • Explored price indicators and used statistical methods to investigate possible relationships.
  • Considered the limits of drawing causal conclusions from observed economic movements.
  • Organized the investigation around questions a nontechnical audience could understand.
