THE BUSINESS QUESTION

What should a company predict, when should the prediction be made, and which variables are available at that point?

Practiced moving from a business question to a target, appropriate input features, and data checks before modeling.

WHAT I WORKED ON

Approach and responsibilities

  • Inspected a small instructional customer dataset and checked structure, missing values, and customer segments.
  • Defined a future churn label as the prediction target and selected explanatory features relevant to customer behavior.
  • Separated treatment-related information from eligible predictive features to avoid data leakage.
  • Documented the analytic workflow so that the business question, rows, features, and timing stayed aligned.
WHAT IT DEMONSTRATES

Why this work matters

This exercise sharpened my understanding of how trustworthy analytical work starts with data representation and clear decision rules, not just a fitted algorithm.

This was a small classroom exercise, not a production churn model or a claim of predictive accuracy. Graphics are illustrative.
