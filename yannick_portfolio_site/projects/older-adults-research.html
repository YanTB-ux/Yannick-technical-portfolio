WHY I CARE

Better quality of life as people age

I am passionate about the well-being of older people and about technology that makes a practical difference in everyday life. My long-term ambition is to contribute to research and eventually develop solutions that help older adults feel safer, more independent, and more connected to the people around them.

WHAT I HOPE TO INVESTIGATE

Ideas for future research

  • How healthcare data might help identify support needs earlier, without treating data as a substitute for human judgment.
  • How accessible digital tools could make everyday tasks easier for older adults and their caregivers.
  • Whether AI could help people stay socially connected and receive timely, useful support.
  • How to design solutions that respect privacy, dignity, affordability, and real-world access to technology.
LOOKING AHEAD

From personal interest to meaningful research

This is still a planned direction rather than an active study. I want to build my skills in business analytics, data engineering, and responsible AI, listen to older adults and caregivers, and develop a focused research question based on real needs. I will share methods, collaborators, and findings only when there is actual work to report.

This is a statement of personal research interest, not a description of a completed project, clinical study, or existing product. No health outcomes or results are being claimed.
